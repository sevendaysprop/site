Switch factor with confidence and clarity

If your not content with your current level of service, changing can be relatively simple process.

Use this six-step route map to gather support, serve notice and onboard 7days without surprises. Every stage is structured, compliant and managed alongside your co-owners.

Guided transition

Step 1 of 6

Review Your Current Factor

Check your factoring agreement, service levels, and notice period to understand how and when a change can be made.

Next
Step 2 of 6

Speak with Your Neighbours

Start informal conversations with other owners to explain why a change is being considered and gauge support.

Back Next
Step 3 of 6

Obtain Owner Agreement

In most tenements, a simple majority of owners is required to appoint or change a property factor.

Back Next
Step 4 of 6

Serve Notice to Current Factor

Formal notice is issued in line with your contract and statutory requirements.

Back Next
Step 5 of 6

Handover & Setup

Records, accounts, and contractor arrangements are transferred smoothly between factors.

Back Next
Step 6 of 6

Welcome Pack & Owner Portal Access

Owners receive documentation, key contacts, and access to an online portal for statements and updates.
