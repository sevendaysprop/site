What this document covers

The Written Statement of Services sets out how we operate as your property factor. It explains our responsibilities, how we communicate with owners, what fees include, complaint handling routes, and the standards we meet under the Property Factors (Scotland) Act.

  • How we procure contractors, manage project budgets, and report back.
