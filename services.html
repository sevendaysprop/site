Services

Find out how we can help you and your property.

Factoring

Managing a building is more than just collecting fees; it’s about ensuring the property is safe, functional, and well-maintained. Our factoring service provides peace of mind by proactively looking after every part of your building.

  • Site inspections to identify issues early
  • Roof and gutter checks to prevent leaks and water damage
  • Grounds and garden maintenance, keeping communal areas tidy
  • Common area oversight, including lighting, security, and general safety

Utilities

Utility bills can be complex, and many large providers often overcharge or miscalculate for multi-unit buildings. At 7days.property, we review all utility accounts carefully, identify errors, and negotiate better rates where possible.

  • Audit existing utility bills to find discrepancies
  • Renegotiate with providers to secure fairer rates
  • Implement cost-saving strategies across the building
  • Ensure transparent billing so everyone in the building benefits

Building Insurance

Building insurance is a legal requirement for all shared properties, protecting residents against damage and liability. 7days.property works with trusted insurance partners to get the best RCA deals and ensure complete coverage.

  • Comprehensive coverage for structural and communal areas
  • Legal compliance for all residents and owners
  • Negotiation with insurers to obtain competitive rates
  • Guidance on claims and risk management

Energy efficiency and retrofit

Retrofit can feel like a big leap, especially for older or heritage tenements. We help you understand what is realistic for your building and how to take steps in the right order.

  • Plain-English overview: what options exist and why they matter.
  • Pros and cons: comfort, disruption, cost ranges, and maintenance impact.
  • Phased approach: start with "no-regrets" measures before major works.
  • Decision support: what owners typically need to approve and when.

Read more about energy efficieny and retrofit-ready options on our retrofit page.
