Full factoring services

We manage the day-to-day work that keeps shared buildings running smoothly. You get clear updates, simple choices, and a practical plan for repairs—without chasing multiple contractors yourself.

  • Assessment: we review issues, gather details, and confirm responsibilities.
  • Repairs: we source contractors, request quotes, and manage work on site.
  • Project coordination: we plan timelines, access, and communication.
  • Insurance liaison: we support claims with evidence and progress updates.

Our online portal keeps everything in one place—updates, documents, and next steps— so owners always know what’s happening.

Insurance and utility savings

Shared-building costs can creep up over time. We look for practical savings that do not reduce cover or service quality. The goal is fewer surprises, better value, and clearer options for owners.

  • Insurance review: we check sums insured, excess levels, and policy gaps.
  • Risk improvements: we flag small changes that can reduce claims and premiums.
  • Claims-ready records: we keep reports, evidence, and repair history organised.
  • Utility deals: we compare shared supplies (e.g., stair lighting) and billing setups.

You’ll receive an easy summary of recommended changes, expected impact, and any trade-offs. Where owners need to vote, we provide a clear decision note so the group can move quickly.

Retrofit-readiness

Retrofit can feel like a big leap, especially for older or heritage tenements. We help you understand what is realistic for your building and how to take steps in the right order.

  • Plain-English overview: what options exist and why they matter.
  • Pros and cons: comfort, disruption, cost ranges, and maintenance impact.
  • Phased approach: start with "no-regrets" measures before major works.
  • Decision support: what owners typically need to approve and when.

We translate the essentials into clear actions, so owners can agree priorities and plan upgrades alongside normal maintenance and repair cycles. Our survey partners can carry out a full inspection of your building, using state-of-the-art methods and equipment, in advance of any decision on major works.
