About 7days

A new approach to the property management sector

Our story

7days.property are a small team of property management professionals and technology specialists, committed to bringing sustainability and efficiency to the long-maligned property factoring sector.

Alongside developing an efficient and customer-focused service around tenement and block management, we work closely with built-environment experts and innovators to meet the future demands of shared building maintenance.

Our approach

Every group of properties has its own requirements. 7days.property offers a modern, responsive property management service, working closely with you to tailor services to specific needs and using a trusted list of contractors.

We treat our clients’ properties as if they were our own, with a strong commitment to exceptional service, cost control, and identifying long-term energy and sustainability benefits.
